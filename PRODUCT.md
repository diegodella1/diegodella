# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are founders, operators, builders, media leaders, and AI-adjacent executives who already have a strong product, idea, or body of work but feel the market is not understanding it, trusting it, or comparing it on the right terms.

They arrive with high cognitive load. They are not looking for a blog archive first. They need a sharp lens, a credible person, and a fast answer to: "Does Diego understand the problem I am living through, and can he help me make it legible?"

## Product Purpose

This site exists to turn Diego Dell'Agostino's work into a monetizable authority platform.

The central public idea is: **The market decides before the search begins.**

The site should make that idea memorable, prove that Diego owns a serious system around it, and create paths into advisory, strategic sprints, talks, workshops, a book, and low-frequency audience building. Success is not page depth alone. Success is qualified trust: the right reader should either start with the thesis, contact Diego, or remember the idea well enough to repeat it.

## Brand Personality

Clear, incisive, original.

The brand should feel like a strategic thinker who can name what others are sensing but cannot yet explain. It should be intellectually serious without becoming academic, commercially useful without sounding like a consulting template, and distinctive without becoming strange for its own sake.

Voice is direct, conceptual, and memorable. It should use simple sentences to carry deep claims.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like:

- A dense archive for insiders.
- A generic advisor or consultant website.
- A SaaS landing page with identical cards, vague benefits, and fake momentum.
- An AI-gradient brand with purple-blue glow, glass panels, and decorative abstraction.
- A literary magazine that hides the commercial purpose.
- A brutalist experiment that is memorable but hard to understand.
- A portfolio that shows taste without explaining the offer.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the idea, not the inventory.
2. Make Diego legible before making the archive exhaustive.
3. Turn concepts into doors: each framework must answer a reader problem.
4. Preserve authority through restraint, but never at the cost of clarity.
5. Every page should move the reader toward one of three actions: understand the thesis, read the right framework, or contact Diego.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic structure, and responsive readability.

Respect reduced-motion preferences. Motion can support authority and pacing, but no content should depend on animation to become visible.

Copy should avoid insider-only language in primary navigation, headings, CTAs, and form labels. Deeper pages can carry more conceptual density once the reader has opted in.
