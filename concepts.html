Definitions
Core concepts and canonical pages
Each card gives one stable definition, one practical use case, and a single canonical destination for deeper reading.
CMOI
Continuous Moment of Intent
A model for how decisions are shaped before explicit search, comparison, or self-aware intent. It explains why many winning decisions are effectively made before the buyer thinks they are deciding.
Best for product clarity, pre-search influence, category framing, and understanding why adoption can fail before a fair evaluation occurs.
Canonical page: thesis
Visibility failure
Occlusion Bias
A model for hidden visibility failure in which products, ideas, or people never arrive at the comparison surface where merit could actually matter.
Best for launches, distribution problems, broken reach, and cases where the team misdiagnoses a visibility failure as a product failure.
Canonical page: occlusion-bias
CAX
Constitutive Arc
A model for how repetition, mediated exposure, and field design shape meaning over time. It describes how audiences are formed, not just reached.
Best for media strategy, authority building, audience transformation, and understanding why narrative outcomes compound through systems rather than single messages.
Canonical page: zmox
Launch foundations
Strategic Debt
Clarity deferred at the start of a product or company compounds into downstream messaging, positioning, and go-to-market problems.
Best for founders with strong product but persistent confusion, incoherent GTM, or repeated re-explanation costs.
Canonical page: paper-01
Market interpretation
Message-Market Fit
The alignment between how a product is interpreted and the conditions under which the market can recognize its value. Product-market fit can exist without message-market fit.
Best for launches that feel unfairly ignored, products that get misunderstood, and teams that solved the thing but not the read of the thing.
Canonical page: paper-02
Organizational coherence
Narrative Drift
The erosion of strategic coherence as organizations scale, fragment, and translate themselves through multiple teams, channels, and intermediaries.
Best for companies that once sounded sharp and now sound generic, inconsistent, or internally misaligned.
Canonical page: paper-04
GTM conditions
Launching Into Noise
The argument that many modern launch playbooks assume functional distribution conditions that no longer exist. Good work now enters a noisier, more mediated field than the old templates assume.
Best for launches that followed the playbook and still disappeared, especially when the product is fine but the context is hostile.
Canonical page: paper-05
Trust and source
Origin Gravity
The tendency for trust, attention, and meaning to re-concentrate around perceived source credibility when mediated environments become saturated or synthetic.
Best for AI-mediated trust, source reputation, authorship, and why origin matters more as synthetic output scales.
Canonical page: origin-gravity
