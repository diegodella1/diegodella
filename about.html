Diego Dell'Agostino

I build media companies, products, and operating systems.

Product, media, and operations executive based in Buenos Aires. Co-founder of Posta, acquired by PodX. Founding media architect and product operations lead at Roxom TV. Author of Narrative Mechanics.

Why this work exists.

For 15+ years I have worked across media, technology, product, marketing, and operations, usually in places where the map was not fully drawn yet.

My work sits between strategy and execution: turning ideas into products, teams, workflows, editorial systems, content operations, distribution loops, technical processes, and internal tools that can actually run.

The recurring pattern was clear: many organizations do not lose because the work is weak. They lose because the product, team, market, and operating system are not coherent enough for the value to become legible.
