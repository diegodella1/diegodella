Background

I have spent over a decade building, scaling, and exiting digital media companies across Latin America. Before founding my own, I worked in digital marketing agencies, SEO, and ad networks (the operational side of how attention is bought, measured, and converted). That experience gave me a perspective I have never been able to shake: the gap between what media organizations think they are building and what the market actually experiences.

At Posta, Argentina's first podcast production company, my partners and I scaled from two people into a regional operation producing original IP, investigative journalism, and thousands of original episodes for clients including Netflix, Uber, Microsoft, HBO, PlayStation, Spotify, and over 75 global brands. We won production, journalism, and advertising awards, were selected for the ICFJ/SembraMedia Velocidad accelerator, and Posta has been part of PodX Group since 2022.

This is a practitioner's body of work — a formalization of patterns I have observed, built on, and sometimes gotten wrong over years of execution.

The work collected here emerged from a specific frustration: most frameworks for media growth and product strategy treat communication as a downstream activity (something you do after the product is built, after the market is identified, after the architecture is fixed). My experience across media, audio, and financial news convinced me the opposite is true, and that the cost of getting it backwards is higher than most organizations realize until it is too late to fix cheaply.

Right now, I am putting those lessons to work in a new venture, applying interpretive authority (not distribution volume) as the primary growth variable. The frameworks here are not theoretical conclusions. They are the questions I work with every day.

Writing

Two bodies of work, one underlying conviction. The frameworks address the question of why some media organizations build lasting authority and others don't. The essays address the more operational question of what that means for how you sequence the work of building.
