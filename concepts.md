# Narrative Mechanics Concepts

Canonical definitions and entry routes for the main Narrative Mechanics concepts.

## Start Here

- If your product is strong but adoption is weak: start with [The Continuous Moment of Intent](https://diegodella.ar/thesis.html), then move to [Strategic Debt](https://diegodella.ar/paper-01.html) and [Message-Market Fit](https://diegodella.ar/paper-02.html).
- If the problem is broken reach and unfair comparison: start with [Occlusion Bias](https://diegodella.ar/occlusion-bias.html).
- If the problem is authority, media systems, and repeated exposure: start with [CAX / The Constitutive Arc](https://diegodella.ar/zmox.html).
- If the problem is launch conditions under modern distribution: start with [Launching Into Noise](https://diegodella.ar/paper-05.html).

## Definitions

### Continuous Moment of Intent (CMOI)

A model for how decisions are shaped before explicit search, comparison, or self-aware intent. It explains why many winning decisions are effectively made before the buyer thinks they are deciding.

Canonical page: [thesis.html](https://diegodella.ar/thesis.html)

### Occlusion Bias

A model for hidden visibility failure in which products, ideas, or people never arrive at the comparison surface where merit could actually matter.

Canonical page: [occlusion-bias.html](https://diegodella.ar/occlusion-bias.html)

### Constitutive Arc (CAX)

A model for how repetition, mediated exposure, and field design shape meaning over time. It describes how audiences are formed, not just reached.

Canonical page: [zmox.html](https://diegodella.ar/zmox.html)

### Strategic Debt

Clarity deferred at the start of a product or company compounds into downstream messaging, positioning, and go-to-market problems.

Canonical page: [paper-01.html](https://diegodella.ar/paper-01.html)

### Message-Market Fit

The alignment between how a product is interpreted and the conditions under which the market can recognize its value. Product-market fit can exist without message-market fit.

Canonical page: [paper-02.html](https://diegodella.ar/paper-02.html)

### Narrative Drift

The erosion of strategic coherence as organizations scale, fragment, and translate themselves through multiple teams, channels, and intermediaries.

Canonical page: [paper-04.html](https://diegodella.ar/paper-04.html)

### Launching Into Noise

The argument that many modern launch playbooks assume functional distribution conditions that no longer exist.

Canonical page: [paper-05.html](https://diegodella.ar/paper-05.html)

### Origin Gravity

The tendency for trust, attention, and meaning to re-concentrate around perceived source credibility when mediated environments become saturated or synthetic.

Canonical page: [origin-gravity.html](https://diegodella.ar/origin-gravity.html)
