6. **AI Agents Need Editorial Systems, Not Just Prompts** — Reliable agents depend on roles, authority, sources, approvals, logs, exceptions, and recovery paths.

8. **Why Community Is Harder to Automate Than Content** — Machines can scale output and coordination, but trust, reciprocity, governance, and belonging remain relational work.
9. **From Publishing Workflows to Agentic Workflows** — The transition is not a prompt upgrade; it is a redesign of state, responsibility, review, and escalation across the publishing system.
10. **Audience, Users, and Community Are Different Systems** — Each relationship has different behaviors, rights, metrics, and product requirements, and confusing them produces bad strategy.
