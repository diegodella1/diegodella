The system

Four ways markets decide early.

The frameworks are not separate essays. They describe one sequence: intent forms, visibility fails, meaning compounds, and launches either survive mediation or disappear.

Intent The Continuous Moment of Intent

Use this when the buyer seems to decide before the visible decision. It explains pre-search influence and latent demand formation.

 Visibility Occlusion Bias

Use this when strong work never reaches fair comparison. It separates product failure from hidden access failure.

 Meaning The Constitutive Arc

Use this when authority, interpretation, and audience memory matter more than one-time reach.

 Launch Launching Into Noise

Use this when standard go-to-market playbooks assume distribution conditions that no longer exist.

Read in order if the product is strong but the market is not understanding it.
