# About Diego Dell'Agostino

Canonical page: <https://diegodella.ar/about.html>

Diego Dell'Agostino writes Narrative Mechanics, a publication on narrative strategy, attention economics, AI, media systems, product clarity, and authority formation.

## Background

- Co-founder of Posta, acquired by PodX Group in 2022
- Builder and operator across digital media, product, and growth in Latin America
- Focused on why good products stall, how mediated systems reshape trust, and how narrative coherence compounds or breaks inside organizations
