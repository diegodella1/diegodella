Narrative Mechanics / category language, mediated trust, filtered discovery

The market forgets strong products when nobody can retell them.

This is an editorial system for founders, strategists, and media operators who need sharper language around category design, authority, AI-shaped visibility, and the pre-search forces that determine what gets remembered, compared, and chosen.

Editorial thesis
Good work disappears when the market never learns the right language for it.

The site studies the layer before conversion metrics: the phrases, filters, and proxies that make one product legible and leave another invisible.
