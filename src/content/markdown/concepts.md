# Narrative Mechanics Concepts

Definitions of the main Narrative Mechanics concepts, with links to the proposals and arguments that introduce them.

## Start Here

- If your product is strong but adoption is weak: start with [{{article.thesis.title}}](https://diegodella.ar/thesis.html), then move to [Strategic Debt](https://diegodella.ar/paper-01.html) and [{{article.paper-02.title}}](https://diegodella.ar/paper-02.html).
- If the problem is broken reach and unfair comparison: start with [{{article.occlusion-bias.title}}](https://diegodella.ar/occlusion-bias.html).
- If the problem is authority, media systems, and repeated exposure: start with [CAX / {{article.zmox.title}}](https://diegodella.ar/zmox.html).
- If the problem is launch conditions under modern distribution: start with [{{article.paper-05.title}}](https://diegodella.ar/paper-05.html).

## Definitions

### Continuous Moment of Intent (CMI)

CMI proposes that useful, repeated encounters with an editorial perspective can shape interpretation and encourage a habit of return before an explicit search or decision.

Canonical page: [thesis.html](https://diegodella.ar/thesis.html)

### {{article.occlusion-bias.title}}

{{article.occlusion-bias.title}} describes the error of judging available work from the selected sample a distribution system made visible.

Canonical page: [occlusion-bias.html](https://diegodella.ar/occlusion-bias.html)

### Constitutive Arc (CAX)

{{article.zmox.title}} proposes a way to design and assess what readers learn through sustained contact with an editorial source.

Canonical page: [zmox.html](https://diegodella.ar/zmox.html)

### Strategic Debt

Clarity deferred at the start of a product or company compounds into downstream messaging, positioning, and go-to-market problems.

Canonical page: [paper-01.html](https://diegodella.ar/paper-01.html)

### {{article.paper-02.title}}

The alignment between how a product is interpreted and the conditions under which the market can recognize its value. Product-market fit can exist without message-market fit.

Canonical page: [paper-02.html](https://diegodella.ar/paper-02.html)

### {{article.paper-04.title}}

The erosion of strategic coherence as organizations scale, fragment, and translate themselves through multiple teams, channels, and intermediaries.

Canonical page: [paper-04.html](https://diegodella.ar/paper-04.html)

### {{article.paper-05.title}}

A proposal to separate acquisition source from evidence of fit, observe early use, and compare cohorts before scaling a launch.

Canonical page: [paper-05.html](https://diegodella.ar/paper-05.html)

### {{article.origin-gravity.title}}

{{article.origin-gravity.title}} proposes that an encounter with useful work can give someone a reason to seek and return to its source. The source may be an individual writer or an accountable editorial organization.

Canonical page: [origin-gravity.html](https://diegodella.ar/origin-gravity.html)
