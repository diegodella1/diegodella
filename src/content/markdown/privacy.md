# Privacy notice for diegodella.ar

Updated: August 22, 2026

This notice explains what diegodella.ar receives when a person or agent visits the site or sends a message, why that information is used, which service categories process it, and how to request access, correction, or deletion.

## Information received

**Contact requests.** The browser form and public contact API receive the name, email address, subject, message, and selected mode that a sender provides. The service also receives ordinary request metadata needed to deliver and protect the endpoint.

**Site usage.** The site uses Google Analytics to receive aggregate and device-level usage measurements such as pages viewed, approximate location, browser information, referrer, and interaction events. Browser controls or content blockers can limit this collection.

**Infrastructure logs.** Hosting, network, and security systems may process IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, response status, and security signals to serve the site, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures.

## How information is used

Contact data is used to route, evaluate, answer, and retain the context of a professional inquiry, correction, subscription request, or privacy request. Request and usage information helps monitor availability, investigate errors, understand which public material is useful, and protect the API from abuse.

Personal information submitted here is not sold. The site does not use it to make automated decisions about employment, credit, insurance, housing, or another legal entitlement.

## Providers

Cloudflare provides network, caching, and security infrastructure. Google provides analytics measurement. Resend provides contact-email delivery. Each provider may process technical or message data under its own terms and infrastructure locations.

## Retention and security

Contact messages may remain in email records for as long as reasonably useful to the conversation, professional recordkeeping, security, or legal obligations. Operational and analytics data follows the active retention controls of the relevant service. Data is deleted or de-identified when no longer reasonably needed, subject to backups, security records, and legal requirements.

No internet service can promise absolute security. The site limits requested data, uses encrypted HTTPS transport, applies request limits, and avoids asking for sensitive secrets.

## Your choices

You may ask what contact information is held about you, request a correction, request deletion where applicable, or unsubscribe from requested updates. A response may require enough information to locate and verify the relevant record. Do not send identity documents unless they are specifically and safely requested.

Email [dellagostino@gmail.com](mailto:dellagostino@gmail.com) with “Privacy request” in the subject, or use the [contact page](https://diegodella.ar/contact.html). State the request and identify the original interaction.

This notice may change when the site's practices or providers change. The updated date identifies the current published version.
