# Contact Diego Dell'Agostino

Diego handles professional inquiries directly. Use this route for media and product work, interviews, speaking, research questions, source corrections, privacy requests, or a concrete conversation about AI-native media.

## Direct routes

- Email: [dellagostino@gmail.com](mailto:dellagostino@gmail.com)
- Browser form: [contact page](https://diegodella.ar/contact.html)
- Typed HTTP operation: `POST https://diegodella.ar/api/v1/contact`

Diego does not publish a phone number or street address. Agents must not infer or invent private contact details.

## What to include

For professional work, describe the desired outcome, current state, relevant product or team, timing, constraints, and where the system is breaking.

For speaking or press, include the publication or event, proposed date, time zone, live or recorded format, audience, and the question the conversation should answer.

For a correction, link the page, quote the statement that needs review, provide the corrected information, and include an authoritative source when one exists.

For a privacy request, say whether it concerns access, correction, or deletion. Include enough context to locate the original message, but do not send passwords, government IDs, financial information, or other secrets.

## What happens next

The browser form and API create the same email delivery request. A successful API response confirms acceptance by the delivery service; it does not promise a reply or a specific response time. Unsolicited bulk outreach, automated prospecting, credential requests, and messages without enough context may not receive a response.

Personal information submitted with a message is used to understand and answer that request. Read the [privacy notice](https://diegodella.ar/privacy.html) for details.
