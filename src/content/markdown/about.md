## Products I'm working on

You can try these products or open their public repositories.

- [PRICEB.TC](https://priceb.tc/): creates free live Bitcoin price widgets for websites and transparent OBS overlays in more than 160 fiat currencies.
- [F1 Race Monitor](https://github.com/diegodella1/f1-race-monitor): a local-first race engineer for EA SPORTS F1 25, with live timing, strategy, race radio, and car health on a phone or tablet beside the wheel.
- [F1 Companion](https://f1.diegodella.ar/): turns Formula 1 race-weekend timing into context on battles, pace, tyres, and strategy.
- [SIGNAL](https://signal.diegodella.ar/): combines independent music, visual culture, and brief updates in a browser-based radio experience.
- [XPoster](https://xposter.diegodella.ar/): monitors authorized sources, detects relevant events, and prepares evidence-linked drafts while editors retain final control.
- [Referí](https://referi.diegodella.ar/): a pocket referee booth for live football matches where you review the play, make the call, and compare it with the official ruling.
- [LiveOverlay](https://liveoverlay.diegodella.ar/): live graphics for QR codes, polls, tickers, lower thirds, sponsors, and more from a single OBS Browser Source.
- [Renamer](https://renamer.diegodella.ar/): local-first, reversible renaming for photos, screenshots, and PDFs.
- [Life Match Argentina](https://lifearg.diegodella.ar/): matches the life you want with Argentine cities using comparable evidence, transparent trade-offs, and confidence levels.
- [DigSign](https://digsign.diegodella.ar/): digital signage operations for campaigns, approvals, playlists, screens, and playback monitoring.
- [Intelligenz](https://intelligenz.diegodella.ar/): strategic intelligence agent that turns monitored sources into relevance-ranked daily briefs.
- [DecisionOS](https://audience.diegodella.ar/): synthetic focus groups that surface objections and turn a product proposal into next experiments before building or launching.
- [Broadcast Control Room](https://broadcast-planner.diegodella.ar/): broadcast planning, media preparation, schedule validation, and browser playout for OBS or vMix.
- [telePRO](https://teleprompter.diegodella.ar/): browser-based teleprompter for live production, remote control, rehearsal, and reliable on-camera delivery.
- [PolyBot BTC](https://tradingbot.diegodella.ar/): safety-first research and paper-trading framework for recurring BTC Up/Down prediction markets.
- [El Presidente](https://presidente.diegodella.ar/): a daily game of decisions, consequences, and minigames where you govern for four years and discover what kind of president you were.
