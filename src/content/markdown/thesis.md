## Core claim

{{article.thesis.title}} (CMI) proposes that useful, repeated encounters with an editorial perspective can shape interpretation and encourage a habit of return. Zero Moment of Interpretation (ZMOI) names the encounter in which an issue begins to acquire meaning for a reader. The thesis sets out predictions and limits for testing those proposals.

## Best use

Read this if your product is strong but adoption is weak, if you are trying to understand pre-search influence, or if you need a model for why “better product” alone often fails to win.
