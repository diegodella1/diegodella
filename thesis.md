# The Continuous Moment of Intent

Canonical page: <https://diegodella.ar/thesis.html>

## Core claim

The Continuous Moment of Intent explains how decisions are shaped before explicit search, comparison, or self-aware choice. Buyers do not enter the market as blank evaluators. They arrive already conditioned by repeated exposure, narrative framing, and pre-search interpretation.

## Best use

Read this if your product is strong but adoption is weak, if you are trying to understand pre-search influence, or if you need a model for why “better product” alone often fails to win.
