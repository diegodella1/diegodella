# Occlusion Bias

Canonical page: <https://diegodella.ar/occlusion-bias.html>

## Core claim

Occlusion Bias describes the hidden visibility failure in which products, ideas, or people never arrive at the comparison surface where merit could actually matter. It explains why many launches and companies misdiagnose a distribution failure as a product failure.

## Best use

Read this if your launch disappeared into noise, if reach feels broken, or if the market never seems to compare you on your actual strengths.
